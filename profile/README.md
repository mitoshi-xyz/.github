Every trade on Polymarket, Hyperliquid or Solana gets a brand-new wallet, funded privately through NEAR Intents. The
trade is public; who made it isn't.

- **Shielded ZEC in your browser.** A real Zcash wallet, librustzcash compiled to WebAssembly. No account, no sign-up.
- **A new wallet for every trade.** Funded with one standard amount at a random moment, from the shielded pool.
- **Who sees what, written down.** Every call about your wallets travels over Oblivious HTTP: the relay sees your IP,
  never what you ask. No server holds your keys or your positions.

Built for [ZECATHON](https://thezecathon.com) · Private Markets. *Mitoshi* (見通し) means **outlook**: the view ahead.
